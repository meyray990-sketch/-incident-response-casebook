This case analyzes a multi‑stage identity‑based incident involving suspicious sign‑ins and anonymous IP activity across multiple users in Microsoft Defender XDR. The investigation focuses on validating successful authentications, reviewing MFA and Conditional Access outcomes, and identifying the specific resources accessed by the attacker. Using EntraIdSignInEvents as the primary telemetry source, the analysis confirms that the attacker successfully authenticates using valid credentials, accesses Azure Active Directory through Microsoft 365 Security & Compliance, and performs reconnaissance without escalating privileges or accessing mailboxes. No evidence of lateral movement, identity modification, or data exfiltration is observed. This case demonstrates practical techniques for handling schema‑limited environments, pivoting through identity logs, and constructing a defensible timeline during credential‑based intrusions.
